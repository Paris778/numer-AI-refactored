# Grandmasters & Seasons (Archived Reference)

Archived summary of Numerai's annual prestige system. Details can change each season.

- **Qualification:** 20 **Qualified Rounds** per season (on-time submission + ≥ 1 NMR total at-risk in the round).
- **Season score:** mean of per-round **stake-weighted average (SWA)** scores: `SWA = Σ(score·stake) / Σ(stake)` over **staked** models only (unstaked excluded; missing rounds count as zero).
- **Canon scores** unify metric-version changes: Canon CORR = `CORR20` through round 484, `CORR20v2` from round 485; Canon TC = TC (unchanged).
- **Titles:** Grandmasters (1st), Masters (top 10), Experts (top 100), Researchers (top third), Contributors (middle third), Apprentices (bottom third), Novices (< 20 qualified rounds); multiple titles possible per scoring category.
- **Discord:** connect via the Numerai bot "The Craibinator" to show your highest title as a role.
